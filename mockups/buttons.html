BOARD / EJECT key options

Four treatments for the one button in the cockpit. Hover and press each. Left is the board state, right is the eject state.

A Flat block

Solid fill, dark text, a 3px darker base edge that compresses on press. No glow, no gradient. The most "product" of the four.

B Outline HUD

Transparent with a glowing outline, fills solid on hover. Eject breathes. Feels like the HUD brackets in the sky.

C Split slab

Chamfered corners, angled divider, the amount in its own darker bay. The most esports of the four.

D Chamfered sweep

Dark chamfered plate with a coloured edge; the fill sweeps in from the right on hover. Valorant / Apex style.
